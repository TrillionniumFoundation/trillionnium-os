The old successful NEG-01 remains qualified for its original source tuple. LATE's public classifier and original-reader delegation preserve that history without granting qualification to the new tuple.

Each new case supplies its original `LEASE.json` through the existing `originals` transport. The complete inventory supplies its exact path, byte length, SHA256 and all nine metadata integers. The consumer checks those original bytes against the closure, then checks the lease schema, home, journal, case, nonce, boot, owner request, selected session and profile, package, complete combination descriptor, retirement and predecessor. The consumer derives journal, home and fixture roots from that bound lease. Equal counts with substituted, reordered or reused roots fail. Historical source parents and the nine failed ancestors do not acquire a new lease requirement.

The change is confined to five exact source substitutions: the constant block, the final-prefix function plus its new pure helper, the early binding check, the source-parent exclusion from the current closure-source check, and the callback invocation. The derivation reverses all substitutions and recovers the complete original 243806-byte consumer. Other policy, retirement and historical validation functions are unchanged.
