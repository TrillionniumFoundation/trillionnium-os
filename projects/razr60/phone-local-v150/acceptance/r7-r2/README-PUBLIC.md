# r7-r2 acceptance source boundary

The formal protocol retains all nineteen cases, their budgets, geometry constraints and required layers. The five cancellation, timeout and disconnect cases still require their physical layer; a successful isolated fixture does not close those cases.

The provider and both frozen collectors are unchanged. Support sources bind a new release namespace and combination while preserving exact original capture, native journal, named-action projection, receipt and postcondition checks. Private previous-input and configuration bindings are placeholders in public review copies.

The normal business evidence sequence is preview, capture (which includes export), then collect. An additional export after a successful capture is unnecessary and is rejected by exclusive output creation. This corrects the historical r7 workflow documentation without changing sealed code.

Health requires original capture actor, phone and collector exit codes of zero. The existing business wrapper binds the original actor result but classifies its capture with phone zero; the Root operator also independently requires actual actor zero. A raw report flag alone is insufficient. Failed tasks and incomplete captures stay failures or unconfirmed.

Host fault and host/component subsets are separate evidence layers. Tests ran against the original pinned sources; projected review copies are not claimed to have executed. Physical UI outcomes and whole-protocol counts are reported separately in the release checkpoint.
